## Source semantics established during tracing (each on named games)

1. **Item scope**: one `div.textBroadcast-item[ filter-ready]` per event,
   NEWEST-FIRST (both eras). Free-text commentary items also carry times
   ("9 мин" Коромыслов… 881356) — classification is by phrase, never by
   time presence.
2. **Clock semantics**: play events = cumulative MM:SS; period/game boundary
   lines (Начало/Окончание … периода/игры/овертайма/серии бросков) = WALL
   clock (MSK). Collision proven on 881356: P1 goal at 19:58 cumulative vs
   "Начало 2 периода" 19:58 wall — resolvable only by keying on event text.
3. **Goal lines carry time, running score and strength** in every season
   sampled (2023-2026): `Изменение счета: <Team>. <№. Scorer> , ассистенты: …
   В равенстве/большинстве/меньшинстве.` + `time` element + score block
   (`score-re__num`, status РАВ/БОЛ/МЕН/БУЛ). The scrape-round claim "goal
   lines carry NO time" is WRONG for the lake as fetched (checked all four
   season openers + all 14 GT games; league-wide count in handoff).
   Protocol goals table (`Заброшенные шайбы`: Ш/Пер/Вр/Счет/Сост + scorer +
   on-ice jersey lists both teams) remains the goal authority; text goals are
   the cross-check. Times agreed exactly in all 14 GT games (0 discrepancies).
4. **EN vocabulary**: conceding-net-empty goals carry `В пустые ворота.`
   (881281 59:25, 881725 54:53, 886054 58:00 «В меньшинстве. В пустые
   ворота.» = SH-ENG, 897655 56:27). Scorer's-own-net-empty goals carry
   `С экстра-полевым игроком.` (881270 58:52, 881827 50:42 6v4, 886161
   57:13) — NOT an ENG (Mestis IM analogue), en=False. Jersey-absence in the
   protocol on-ice lists confirms both directions on every named case
   (e.g. 881281 goal 5: ЦСКА column 15,27,44,50,87,89 — goalie 39 absent;
   881270 goal 4: Салават column lacks goalie 1).
5. **Pull/return/substitution**: `<Team>. Замена вратаря на экстра-полевого
   игрока` / `<Team>. Вратарь в воротах` (team-name prefix + club logo tid);
   substitution is the OTHER word order: `Замена вратаря. <Team>. <new>
   вместо <old>` (881261 27:43 Паскуале за Кошечкина; 886161 20:00/40:00
   period-boundary swaps; 889995 46:11; 897536 27:45) — never an interval.
6. **Delayed penalty**: `Отложенный штраф у команды <Team>` — NO time on the
   line; chronological position between neighbours is the only timing. The
   channel is explicit but PARTIAL: games mentioning >=1 dp = 2/748 (2023),
   110/782 (2024), 382/782 (2025), 425/748 (2026).
7. **Penalty begin**: `Удаление. <Team>. <№. Player> . <N> мин. <offense>.`
   (+ optional `Отбывал: <№. Player>`), team variant `Удаление. <Team>.
   Командный штраф . 2 мин. …`. Times exact MM:SS in all seasons. Protocol
   fineTable (Вр/Игрок/Мин/Нарушение per team) mirrors them 1:1 in all 14
   games — including same-second duplicates, which are REAL (below).
8. **Penalty end events are corroboration only**: `Команда <Team> играет в
   полном составе` (2026), `<Team> играет в полном составе` (2024),
   `Команда "<Team>" в полном составе.` (2025), name variants («Куньлунь Ред
   Стар» for Куньлунь РС, 889939), minute-only times in 2023/2024 ("07 мин"),
   sometimes NO time (881270). Ends are therefore COMPUTED (windows below),
   with the exact-time 2025/2026 events used as spot corroboration — every
   computed release in the GT set that has an exact-time event agrees to the
   second (881725 10:51/24:51, 897536 26:00/40:41, 897655 33:58/34:55/30:12).
9. **Penalty shot**: award `Буллит (<Team>). Наказан. <№. Player> . <offense>.`
   = protocol row with **minutes 0** (881270 19:13, 886161 05:49) — no box
   time. Missed = `Не удалось реализовать штрафной бросок`; post-game SO =
   `Послематчевый буллит: …` rows (no time) + decision row `Изменение счета:
   … Буллит.` at 65:00 = protocol goals row Пер=`РБ`/Сост=`бул.` — excluded
   from goals, and its running-score increment must be skipped knowingly
   (889995). OT goals: protocol Пер=`1 ОТ` → period 4 (881270 63:35).
10. **Same-second duplicate penalty rows are REAL (double minors), not feed
   dupes** — the scrape session's "dedupe" note is REFUTED by the protocol
   team ВПМ/ВПБ tables on both named games:
   - 898094 51:57 Бреус ×2 «Игра высоко поднятой клюшкой»: Барыс returns to
     full strength at 55:57 (= +4:00) and Барыс P3 ВПМ 6:41 decomposes ONLY
     as 0:55 (командный carryover) + 1:46 (Маккошен, PP-goal-released) +
     4:00 (Бреус 2+2).
   - 881261 43:28 Минулин ×2 «Грубость» (one row `Отбывал: Голдобин`): ЦСКА
     P3 ВПМ 1:34 = Соркин-2 58:26→horn alone, and Металлург P3 ВПМ 2:06 =
     6s (43:28→43:34 goal) + Фисенко 2:00 — only consistent with TWO real
     Минулин minors (one cancelling Соркин's, one creating the PP that the
     43:34 goal ended). 2+2 to one player also at 889859 31:49 (Джозефс),
     889939 34:39 (Бокун), 897655 31:32 (Томпсон).
11. **Box-strength model** (adapter-level; verified against team ВПМ/ВПБ
    tables on 881261/881270/881725/898094 exactly, corroborated on
    881827/897536/897655):
    - misconducts (>=10 min incl. 20-min disciplinary) never shorthand a team
      (881261 P1: ЦСКА ВПМ 9:00 = 2+5+2 with the 20 excluded);
    - coincident penalties of equal length cancel pairwise across teams at
      the same second (no box strength): 881270 34:14 2+2 vs 2+2 full cancel
      (ВПМ exact), 898094 33:05 2v2 → «Игра 4 на 4», 881356 44:34 pair not
      released by the 46:19 goal (both expired naturally at 46:34);
    - same-player multiple minors at one second are served CONSECUTIVELY
      (stacked windows);
    - a goal by a team whose opponent is NET-shorthanded releases the
      earliest-ending ACTIVE remaining minor (881725 Шинкевич 09:57→10:51
      = ВПБ 0:54; Меркли →24:51 = ВПБ 4:29 exact; 881281 Кагарлицкий
      →15:24 = ВПБ 2:26 exact; 889859 Джозефс stacked pair released in its
      SECOND window at 33:57; 897536 26:00/40:41; 897655 34:55);
      majors never release (881261 Полтапов 5-min ran through the 13:54 PP
      goal; 897655 Колесников 30:12 = +5:00 exact); no release at equal
      boxes (881827 18:26 goal at 1-1).
12. **Momentary dp-pattern pulls** (net briefly empty, ends at a whistle):
    outside P3 gap-3 windows they are plain intervals (881270 05:13-05:19 &
    08:24-08:27; 886054 01:15-01:23; 897536 16:11-16:22 with explicit dp
    event; 881281 31:53-32:15 by the LEADING team). Inside instances the
    shared engine's rulings 17/17b decide (three GT cases below). Rulings
    17/17b are NOT modified (kickoff order); the explicit dp events go to the
    aux export for the Manager's cross-calibration.
13. **Coaches**: text-page preview frame `preview-frame__club-nameTrainer`
    (left = home, right = away; left club name/logo always = title's first
    team). Format drift: 2023/2024 «Фамилия Имя», 2025/2026 «Фамилия Имя
    Отчество» — the same person appears as «Никитин Игорь» (2024) and
    «Никитин Игорь Валерьевич» (2025): cross-season identity = handoff
    adjudication (adapter emits verbatim). Foreign coaches short («Айрленд
    Грег», «Буше Ги»).
14. **Goalie numbers** (for EN jersey-absence resolution): protocol «Вратари»
    player tables, first column №; starting goalies also on the `Начало 1
    периода В воротах: …` line.
